Same engine, same day. Two ways to face it.

Both directions run on the same rules and the same seed data: a six-person design studio, five things due today. What changes is who the screen is built for. Every board is live — clear actions, open people, log notes — and each flips to its empty state.

A

The Spine

Optimizes for the person who checks in once or twice a day: one calm list, read top to bottom, where the shape of each mark tells you why it's there and the day burns down as you clear it.

Trade-off: at thirty-plus actions it's a long scroll with no fast lane — it asks you to read, not to process.

B

Triage desk

Optimizes for the person who owns the relationships and clears the list fast: a dense queue beside a detail pane, driven by J / K / E, built to be emptied in minutes.

Trade-off: it rewards daily habit and punishes the occasional user; to a first-timer on a three-person team it reads as a tool for someone else.
