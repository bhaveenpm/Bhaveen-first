# LogTable

Everything said with one person, newest first: date, type, who spoke, what was said.

- Date, type and who are mono and uppercase; the note is plain `ink` and wraps.
- Who reads "you" in `select` when someone at Halden reached out, otherwise the contact's first name.
- With no entries it shows one row saying what will appear there.
- Consumer provides `entries` and an optional `empty` sentence.
